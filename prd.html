Pacific Aqua Sales System

Full requirements: personas, users, use cases, page details, and functionalities for the mobile sales app and backend admin.

Mobile mockup Backend mockup Raw markdown

1. Problem & goal

Sales live in Excel today. Field entry, multi-user edits, dues, and reporting are painful. Ship two web apps on one ledger: mobile for executives, backend for owner/office. Excel becomes export-only.

4. Use cases

  1. UC-01 Login — any user signs in and lands on role-correct home.
  2. UC-02 Create field sale — executive captures multi-line invoice + payment on phone.
  3. UC-03 Quick-add customer — create dealer/retailer while booking a sale.
  4. UC-04 Today’s performance — home KPIs for own sales/collected/pending.
  5. UC-05 Collect balance — record payment on Partial/Pending sale.
  6. UC-06 Chase dues (office) — admin works dues queue by city/salesperson.
  7. UC-07 Maintain product rates — admin updates catalog; mobile reads new rates.
  8. UC-08 Month close export — filter + download CSV/XLSX like current Excel.
  9. UC-09 Onboard salesperson — admin invites executive user.
  10. UC-10 Void / correct sale — admin voids with reason; audit retained.
  11. UC-11 Language toggle — HI/EN labels on mobile.

5. Functionalities

  • Auth — login, logout, roles, active flag
  • Sales — multi-line invoice, auto PA-####, payment mode/status, share text
  • Customers — create/search/edit; outstanding computed
  • Products — admin CRUD; mobile rate reference
  • Dues — pending/partial queue; record payment history
  • Dashboards — mobile today KPIs; backend month KPIs
  • Reports — filtered Excel/CSV export
  • Users — invite/deactivate executives
  • Localization — Hindi + English on mobile

8. Business rules

  • Invoice numbers unique (PA-####)
  • Status derived from amount paid vs total (Pending / Partial / Paid)
  • Amount paid cannot exceed total
  • Voided sales kept for audit; excluded from revenue KPIs
  • Executives see only their sales on mobile
  • Past sale lines keep rate snapshot if product price changes later
